ParkAlert – the simple parking timer for supermarket and short-stay car parks.

Popped in for a quick shop and lost track of the maximum parking time? ParkAlert reminds you before your parking time runs out – with a warning 10 minutes before and an alarm when it expires.

──────────────────────────────
HOW IT WORKS
──────────────────────────────
1. Open the app – or start ParkAlert directly in Android Auto
2. Select your parking duration: 15, 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes
3. Run your errands – the timer keeps running in the background
4. 10 minutes before expiry: warning
5. When time is up: alarm notification with vibration

──────────────────────────────
FEATURES
──────────────────────────────
✅ 5 quick-select buttons: 15 / 30 / 60 / 90 / 120 minutes
✅ Large, easy-to-read countdown
✅ Colour feedback: Blue (running) → Yellow (10 min left) → Red (expired)
✅ Keeps running with the screen locked
✅ Alarm notification and vibration when time expires
✅ Overtime display: shows how long you have been over the limit
✅ Start and expiry time at a glance
✅ Android Auto: start the timer with one tap when you park
✅ English and German
✅ No account, no registration, no cloud

──────────────────────────────
IDEAL FOR
──────────────────────────────
• Supermarket and customer car parks with a maximum stay
• Parking zones with a parking disc
• Short-stay city centre parking
• Shopping centres and retail parks

──────────────────────────────
PRIVACY
──────────────────────────────
ParkAlert is free and funded by Google AdMob banner ads. On first launch you are asked for your consent (GDPR / IAB TCF 2.2). You can decline – the app keeps working fully with non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.

ParkAlert itself does not collect location data or store any personal data. Google AdMob may process data such as the advertising ID, the IP address and an approximate location derived from it to serve ads. See the privacy policy for details.

No ads are shown in Android Auto.
